Our Story

SOUND
IS ART

Who We Are

Sonic Wave was born from a single, uncompromising belief — that music deserves to be heard exactly as its creators intended it.

Founded by a group of acoustic engineers, audiophiles, and industrial designers in Bangalore, India, Sonic Wave set out to create headphones that didn't force a compromise between performance, aesthetics, and comfort.

The ProSound Elite X1 is the culmination of years of research, hundreds of prototype iterations, and countless listening sessions. Every component has been obsessively refined to deliver an experience that rivals — and in many ways surpasses — headphones costing three times the price.

We believe premium audio shouldn't be the exclusive privilege of those who can afford four-figure price tags. Excellence should be accessible. That's the Sonic Wave promise.

Our Journey

From Idea
to Icon

2018
The Founding Vision

Three acoustic engineers, frustrated by the compromises in the market, sketch the first concepts for what will become Sonic Wave in a Bangalore apartment. The core philosophy: studio sound for everyone.

2019
First Prototypes

After 14 months of R&D, the first functional prototype achieves a frequency response that surprises even the team. Funding is secured from a leading Indian tech venture fund.

2021
Manufacturing Partnership

Sonic Wave partners with a precision audio manufacturer to bring production-grade units to life. Over 200 drivers are tested and rejected before the final 40mm neodymium unit is approved.

2023
Advanced ANC Development

The breakthrough comes: a proprietary multi-microphone ANC algorithm that adapts in real time to ambient noise signatures. Patent pending. The results in testing are remarkable.

2025
ProSound Elite X1 — New Release

The ProSound Elite X1 launches. Everything we've built, tested, and refined for seven years, delivered in a single product that redefines what premium wireless audio means in India and beyond.

Our Principles

Design
Philosophy

01
Acoustic Purity

Every design decision is judged first by how it affects the sound. The driver geometry, enclosure material, and seal quality are optimised for acoustic performance before aesthetics.

02
Enduring Form

We reject trend-driven design. The Elite X1's matte-black industrial aesthetic is timeless — it won't look dated in five years. Restraint is the highest form of design confidence.

03
Human Engineering

Headphones are worn, not just held. Clamping force, weight distribution, ear cup size, and headband pressure all underwent clinical ergonomic analysis and dozens of fit trials.
