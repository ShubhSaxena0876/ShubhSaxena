What's Inside

Every Feature,
Engineered to Excel

40mm Premium Drivers

Custom neodymium drivers deliver full-range audio with astonishing precision. Frequency response of 10Hz–22kHz reproduces every nuance of your music.

Advanced ANC

Multi-microphone adaptive noise cancellation suppresses ambient sound up to 35dB across all frequencies. Three ANC modes: Maximum, Wind Block, and Ambient.

40-Hour Battery

Industry-leading 40-hour playback with ANC off. 30 hours with ANC active. Quick Charge delivers 5 hours of playback from a 10-minute charge via USB-C.

Bluetooth 5.3

Latest generation Bluetooth with multipoint connection supports two devices simultaneously. Stable 10-metre range with aptX HD and AAC codec support.

Rapid Charge Technology

ProSound's proprietary charging circuit safely delivers maximum current while protecting battery longevity. Full charge from 0 in under 2 hours via USB-C.

Ergonomic Design

Protein leather memory foam ear cushions and a self-adjusting headband distribute weight evenly. Tested for 8+ hours of fatigue-free listening.

Touch Controls

Capacitive touch panel on the right earcup handles volume, playback, call management, and ANC toggling. Works even with light gloves.

Crystal-Clear Calls

Four microphones with beamforming AI isolate your voice from background noise. Voice calls sound professional even in busy environments.

Premium Build Quality

Aircraft-grade aluminium adjustment slider, reinforced nylon-fibre headband, and a matte-carbon polycarbonate shell built to withstand years of daily use.
